# Lessons for KOML

Lightweight SOWs need explicit acceptance criteria as much as a fee and scope. Missing acceptance is detectable from inputs, while whether a client will accept the work cannot be inferred.

Synthetic evidence validates integer fee reconciliation and exact identification of planted review cases. The assumption that this saves real owner time remains untested. No customer demand or two-week use evidence exists yet.

Operating rule: separate observable contract facts from legal judgement and commercial outcomes. Require explicit dates, provenance and integer money, then compare exact issue identities against independent ground truth before claiming accuracy.

An initial determinism test exposed state files inside input directories being rejected on the second run. The loader now excludes the explicitly selected state path, retaining strict rejection of unrelated unsupported files. This is an evidence-based build correction, separate from the pending real-use improvement hook.
